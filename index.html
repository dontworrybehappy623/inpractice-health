InPractice Health is a data infrastructure platform for the aesthetic and longevity medicine space. We help clinics monetize their existing clinical data and give data partners access to compliant, de-identified real-world evidence. If you run a clinic or need clinical data to build products, we’re built for you.

We use the HIPAA Expert Determination method, a certified statistical approach to de-identification. This means patient privacy is mathematically guaranteed before any data leaves the clinic’s control.

We work with data already generated in practice: biomarkers, diagnostics, treatment protocols, medication usage, and longitudinal patient outcomes. We integrate with your existing EMR, POS, and CRM systems.

Absolutely. Clinics retain full custody and ownership of their data. InPractice Health acts as a processing and distribution layer — you decide what gets shared, and you earn revenue from every dataset produced.
