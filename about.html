About

InPractice Health is a data infrastructure platform for the aesthetic and longevity medicine space. We turn existing clinical data into non-patient revenue for clinics and real-world evidence for data partners.

◆ Our Vision

Building the neutral data layer for aesthetic medicine

We believe the most impactful clinical data in aesthetic and longevity medicine is locked inside individual clinic systems. InPractice Health creates the infrastructure to unlock that value — compliantly, transparently, and on the clinic’s terms.

The most valuable data in aesthetic medicine never leaves the clinic — until now. We’re building the bridge between clinical practice and real-world evidence, where every clinic benefits and every data partner gets compliant access.

⚙ How It Works

A neutral layer built on trust

InPractice Health connects directly to existing clinic systems — EMRs, POS platforms, and CRMs — without requiring any workflow changes. Data is de-identified using HIPAA-compliant expert determination before it ever leaves the clinic.

Clinics retain full custody and ownership of their data. When that data generates value through our partner network, clinics earn revenue share. We are not an EMR, not a POS, not a CRM, and not a dashboard — we are the neutral infrastructure layer that makes existing data work harder for everyone.
